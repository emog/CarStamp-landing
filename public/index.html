Car expense tracker for iPhone and iPad, also on Mac: Every mile of your car, on the record.

CarStamp keeps itemized service invoices, fuel and charging, and reminders for inspection, insurance, registration or road tax and servicing, and shows what each mile or km really costs. One history, ready for the next owner. Free for one car, with no account and no ads.

Private: no account, no ads, no data collected.

Moving from Fuelio, Drivvo, Fuelly, aCar or Road Trip? Bring your history over, free, with no column mapping.

What CarStamp keeps

  • Itemized services

    Parts and labor line by line, as on the invoice, scanned or typed.

  • Fuel and charging

    Gasoline, diesel, LPG, CNG, hydrogen or kWh, even gasoline and LPG in one car, in any currency. Fuel use is worked out for each fuel.

  • Maintenance and renewals

    The factory plan, inspection, insurance and registration or road tax, reminded by date or distance.

  • A buyer-ready history

    A file to hand over when you sell, and with Pro a service history PDF to share or print.

Upcoming

Know what is due next.

Maintenance by date or distance, inspection, insurance and registration or road tax, with a reminder before each one and how many days or miles are left. Mark it done straight from the notification.

Services

Every invoice, line by line.

Parts with their brand, part number and where you bought them, labor and the workshop, as on the invoice, or marked as done yourself. A warranty repair at zero cost still belongs in the history.

Service history PDF

A history a buyer can trust.

Every service with its parts, part numbers and labor, the maintenance plan and the renewals, in one PDF with your name and the receipts attached. Share it with a buyer or a workshop, save it or print it. With Pro.

Documents

Papers with their expiry dates.

Expiry dates and reminders for your registration, insurance and license. Page photos: one document with up to 2 pages free, every document with Pro.

Insights

What the car really costs.

Spending for this year and last, the monthly chart, cost per mile and average fuel use, all free. Pro adds per-tank detail, price history, a forecast and the change against the last period.

Questions

What is the best car maintenance app for iPhone?

It depends on what you want the app to keep. CarStamp keeps fuel and charging, services line by line, expenses and renewals such as insurance, inspection and registration in one app, with its own apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro, no account, no ads and no data collected, and it is free for one car. The CarStamp blog compares eight car maintenance and fuel log apps for iPhone side by side.

What is CarStamp?

CarStamp is an app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro that keeps a complete record of your car: fuel and charging, services and parts, expenses, mileage, maintenance and renewals such as insurance, technical inspection, vignette and road tax, with reminders before each one is due. It works for motorcycles and vans too.

Is CarStamp free?

Yes. CarStamp is free for one car, for good, with unlimited records, every reminder, basic insights, backup, CSV export and Hand Over to New Owner. CarStamp Pro is optional and is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices vary by country and are shown in the App Store.

Which devices does CarStamp run on?

CarStamp runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS and iPadOS 18 or later, on Mac with macOS 15 or later, and on Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 2 or later. Without Pro each device keeps its own data; with Pro your data syncs through your own iCloud.

Does CarStamp work in my country?

Yes. CarStamp works in any country: distances in miles or kilometers, fuel in gallons or liters, fuel use in mpg or L/100 km, and any currency. When you add a car, it suggests the usual renewals (insurance, inspection, registration, road tax or a vignette) for 30 countries, including the U.S., the U.K., Canada and Australia, and a general set everywhere else. You can add your own too.

Does CarStamp collect my data or need an account?

No. CarStamp needs no account, shows no ads and collects no data. Your records stay on your devices, and with Pro they sync through your own iCloud account. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Can I move my data from Fuelio, Drivvo, Fuelly, aCar or Road Trip?

Yes. CarStamp imports the files Fuelio, Drivvo, Fuelly, aCar and Road Trip export, without mapping columns, and any CSV file. Importing is free.
