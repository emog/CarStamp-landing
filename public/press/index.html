Short description

CarStamp is a private car log for iPhone and iPad, with Mac and Apple Vision Pro coming soon. It keeps fuel and charging, itemized services, expenses, mileage and reminders for maintenance and renewals in one place. No account, no ads, no data collected. It works in any country and is free for one car.

Long description

CarStamp is a private car log for iPhone and iPad, with Mac and Apple Vision Pro coming soon, made by an independent developer in Bulgaria. It keeps a car's whole story in one place: fill-ups and charging, from gasoline and LPG to electric and plug-in hybrid; itemized services, as on the invoice; every expense; the mileage; and reminders for maintenance and renewals such as insurance, inspection and registration, by date or distance. It works in any country, in miles or kilometers and any currency, with suggested renewals for 30 countries. No account, no ads, no analytics: the data stays on the device, and with iCloud sync in the owner's own iCloud; receipts are read on the device. When the car is sold, its history can go to the next owner as a file they open in CarStamp. It is free for one car; CarStamp Pro adds more cars, iCloud sync and a service history PDF.

Key features

  • Fuel and charging. Gasoline, diesel, LPG, CNG, hydrogen, electric and plug-in hybrids, even two fuels in one car, with fuel use worked out for each fuel.
  • Services line by line. Parts with their brand, part number and where they were bought, labor and the workshop, as on the invoice, or marked as done yourself.
  • Every expense. Recurring costs such as insurance or a lease are added on their day.
  • Maintenance and renewals. The maintenance plan, inspection, insurance and registration or road tax, with reminders by date or distance. Mark Done or Renew straight from the notification.
  • Receipts and the odometer, read on the device. Scan a receipt and CarStamp reads it. Scan Odometer reads the mileage from the dashboard with the camera on iPhone and iPad.
  • Insights. Spending, cost per mile or km and fuel economy, by month and by category, with the biggest expenses.
  • Documents. Registration, insurance and license with their expiration dates and reminders.
  • Any country. Miles or kilometers; liters, US or imperial gallons, or kWh; any currency, even several in one car. Suggested renewals for cars registered in 30 countries, a general set elsewhere, and custom renewals.
  • The history goes with the car. Hand Over to New Owner sends the buyer the car's history as a file they open in CarStamp, and sending it is free. With Pro, a service history PDF to share, save or print.
  • Your data, your way. Import from other car apps or a CSV file; backup, restore and CSV export are free.
  • Made for each device. Quick actions on the app icon on iPhone and iPad, and in the Dock on Mac. Ready for iPhone Duo, folded and open. On Apple Vision Pro it opens as a window in your space. With Pro: iCloud sync, Siri and Shortcuts, and widgets on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Pricing

Free for one car, for good. Unlimited records, every reminder, documents with expiration dates, 10 receipt reads to start, Scan Odometer, insights for this month, the last 3, 6 or 12 months, this year and last year, import, backup, export and Hand Over to New Owner. No ads and no time limit.

Privacy

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The data stays on the device and, with iCloud sync turned on, in the owner's own iCloud. The app uses no servers of the developer's, and the developer never sees the data.
  • Receipts and the odometer are read on the device.
  • Purchases go through Apple.
  • App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
