What CarStamp does on Android

  • Itemized services

    Parts and labor line by line, as on the invoice. Receipts are read on your device.

  • Maintenance and renewals

    Alerts by date or distance for servicing, inspection, insurance and registration or road tax, with your documents and their expiry dates.

  • Insights

    Spending, cost per mile or km and fuel use. Pro adds per-tank detail and a forecast.

  • Widgets, shortcuts and tiles

    What is due on your Home Screen, quick actions on the app icon and Quick Settings tiles.

  • Sync with Google Drive

    Your cars and records on every Android phone and tablet you use, through your own Google Drive. With Pro.

  • A history for the next owner

    A backup file, CSV export and Hand Over to New Owner, all free. With Pro, a service history PDF.

Questions

How do I move my car’s history from iPhone to Android?

On iPhone, open Settings and choose Back Up; on Android, choose Restore from a Backup. One file carries every car, record and attachment, and it works the other way round too.

Does Google Drive sync need an account?

No CarStamp account. With Pro, turn on Sync with Google Drive in Settings and sign in with your Google Account: your Android phones and tablets stay in step through a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only CarStamp can use.

Does Pro from the App Store work on Android?

No. Pro is bought on Google Play for your Android devices and on the App Store for Apple devices, and a purchase in one store does not carry over to the other. Your records move between them with a backup file.

Does Pro from Google Play work on Apple devices?

No. Pro bought on Google Play works on your Android phones and tablets with the same Google account. On Apple devices, Pro is bought on the App Store, where one purchase covers all of them. Your records still move to an Apple device with a backup file, free.

Can I bring my history from Fuelio, Drivvo or aCar?

Yes. CarStamp reads the exports of Fuelio, Drivvo, Fuelly, aCar and Road Trip without mapping columns, and any other CSV file. Importing is free.

What does CarStamp know about me?

Nothing. There is no account, no ads and no analytics, and the developer runs no servers. Your records stay on your devices and, with sync, in your own Google Drive.
